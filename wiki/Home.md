## 🏛️ Project Overview

**Qmoosa Equality** is an experimental Algorand economic-inclusion platform dedicated to supporting Moolnivasi, SC, ST, OBC, minorities, and underserved communities worldwide.

The project operates under a strict **Reality Gate** protocol:
- **Zero Simulation / Zero Mock**: No synthetic RPC data, fake blockchain confirmations, or mock testing.
- **Fail-Closed Verification**: The system refuses to deploy or certify release readiness unless actual cryptographic proofs and real on-chain transaction confirmations are present.
- **Post-Quantum Security**: Native off-chain manifest signing using NIST FIPS 204 **ML-DSA-65**.
- **Transparent Boundaries**: Pure integer economics, strict dilution caps, and absolute privacy protection (no caste, religious, or identity metadata on-chain).
